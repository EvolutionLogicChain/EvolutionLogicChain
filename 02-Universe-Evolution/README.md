# Universe Evolution

Universe evolution describes the development of physical structures.

## Main Path

Energy

↓

Particles

↓

Atoms

↓

Stars

## Energy

Energy is the foundation of physical change.

## Particles

Particles represent fundamental structures of matter.

## Atoms

Atoms are stable structures formed by particles.

## Stars

Stars are large-scale structures formed through gravity and matter aggregation.
